## What to Look for in a Crypto Analytics App

Not all analytics apps are built the same. Here's what actually separates a useful one from one that just looks good in screenshots.

**On-chain data access.** On-chain data is information recorded directly on a blockchain: wallet flows, transaction volumes, network activity. It tells you what's happening with Bitcoin, Ethereum, or Solana at the network level, not just what the price is doing on an exchange.

**Automation and scheduling.** Manually checking five dashboards every morning is its own kind of noise. The best tools do the work for you, on a schedule you set.

**Cross-asset coverage.** Most serious retail investors hold both crypto and equities. An app that covers only one side forces you to constantly switch.

**Accessibility without a learning curve.** Raw data is only useful if you can act on it. The best apps translate complex signals into clear, personalized insights — no analytical expertise required.

### Glassnode

Glassnode is the most established on-chain analytics platform for Bitcoin and Ethereum. With over 1,500 on-chain metrics and up to 15 years of historical data, it's a serious research tool for investors who want to go deep on blockchain fundamentals.

The depth is genuinely impressive. Miner behavior, long-term holder supply, exchange inflows. If you want to analyze Bitcoin or Ethereum at that level, Glassnode has the data.

But it's a passive dashboard. No agent automation, no scheduled research delivery, no equities coverage. You have to know what you're looking for, find it yourself, and interpret it yourself. The Advanced plan runs $49 per month billed annually, with Professional pricing available on request.

**Best for:** Investors who specialize in Bitcoin and Ethereum on-chain research and are comfortable doing their own interpretation.

**Strengths:** Unmatched on-chain data depth for BTC and ETH, extensive historical dataset.

**Limitations:** Crypto-only, no AI automation, no equities coverage, requires manual analysis.

### Nansen

Nansen focuses on wallet labeling and smart money flow tracking across Ethereum and Solana. Its core value is showing you where sophisticated wallets — labeled as "smart money" — are moving funds. If you want to track which wallets are accumulating a specific token or where capital is flowing across DeFi protocols, Nansen is built for that.

The Pioneer plan runs roughly $120 per year; the Professional plan is $1,200 per year. Its Trustpilot average sits at 2.9 out of 5, which reflects a mixed experience among retail investors.

Like Glassnode, it's crypto-only with no automation layer. The research is still manual.

**Best for:** Investors focused on the Ethereum and Solana ecosystems who want wallet-level intelligence.

**Strengths:** Smart money tracking, wallet labeling, DeFi flow visibility.

**Limitations:** No equities coverage, no agent scheduling, mixed user satisfaction, steep cost at the professional tier.

### Token Metrics

Token Metrics offers AI-generated ratings across more than 6,000 crypto projects and integrates on-chain trade execution through LiFi. It leans more toward execution than research. The AI ratings are useful for quickly screening projects, and the execution layer is a genuine differentiator for active traders.

Pricing starts at approximately $49 per month. But like the others, it's entirely crypto-focused. There is no equities coverage. The kind of scheduled agent automation Agenticly uses doesn't exist here. The AI layer is oriented around scoring and execution, not autonomous recurring research.

**Best for:** Active crypto traders who want AI-assisted project ratings and integrated trade execution.

**Strengths:** Broad project coverage, AI ratings, execution integration.

**Limitations:** Crypto-only, no equities, no scheduled research agents.

### Kaito AI

Kaito AI aggregates social signals from across Web3: X posts, Discord conversations, community sentiment. It's useful for understanding narrative momentum and tracking which topics are gaining traction in crypto communities.

But it's not a structured investment research tool. No on-chain data depth, no equities coverage, no agent automation. It's a sentiment layer, not an analytics platform. It's useful as a complement to other tools, but not a standalone research solution.

**Best for:** Investors who want to track crypto narrative and social momentum.

**Limitations:** No on-chain data depth, no equities coverage, not a research platform.
