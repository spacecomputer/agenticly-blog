## What to Look for in an AI Stock Research App

The right app depends on what you're actually trying to solve. Before diving in, ask yourself:

Do you hold only stocks, only crypto, or both? Do you want passive dashboards or something that runs research automatically? Are you comfortable interpreting raw data, or do you need it translated into plain insights? What's your budget threshold?

Most apps on this list do one or two of these things well. Only one does all of them in a single mobile interface. Keep that in mind as you read.

### 2. Glassnode

**Best for: Serious on-chain analysts focused exclusively on Bitcoin and Ethereum**

Glassnode is the most established on-chain analytics platform for Bitcoin and Ethereum. It offers over 1,500 metrics with up to 15 years of historical data. Miner behavior, long-term holder supply, exchange inflows, realized cap — if you want depth, it's here.

**What it covers:** BTC and ETH on-chain metrics, historical data, charting tools.

**What it costs:** Advanced plan at $49/month (billed annually); Professional pricing is custom.

**Where it falls short:** Crypto-only. No Solana. No US equities. No AI agent automation. You pull the data and interpret it yourself — powerful, but it requires both analytical skill and time.

**Who it's for:** Investors already fluent in on-chain data who want the deepest possible Bitcoin and Ethereum dataset. Not the right fit if you also hold stocks or want automated insights.

### 3. Nansen

**Best for: Tracking smart money wallet flows on Ethereum and Solana**

Nansen built its reputation on wallet labeling — identifying which wallets belong to known funds, exchanges, and high-activity traders, then letting you track where that capital is moving. Its Ethereum and Solana coverage is solid.

**What it covers:** Wallet labeling, token flow tracking, Ethereum and Solana activity.

**What it costs:** Pioneer plan at roughly $120/year; Professional plan at $1,200/year.

**Where it falls short:** No equities coverage. No agent automation. Everything requires manual interpretation, and its Trustpilot average of 2.9 out of 5 reflects a mixed experience among retail investors.

**Who it's for:** Investors who want to follow on-chain wallet activity and are willing to pay for that specific capability. Not suited for anyone who also needs stock market coverage.

### 4. Token Metrics

**Best for: Crypto-focused investors who want AI-generated ratings across a wide project universe**

Token Metrics generates AI ratings across more than 6,000 crypto projects and integrates on-chain trade execution through LiFi. It's built for investors who want a signal layer across a broad crypto universe, not just the top assets.

**What it covers:** AI ratings for 6,000+ crypto projects, on-chain execution integration.

**What it costs:** Starting at approximately $49/month.

**Where it falls short:** Crypto-only. The focus is on execution and ratings rather than scheduled research automation. If you want recurring, personalized research delivered automatically, that's not what Token Metrics is designed for.

**Who it's for:** Crypto-heavy investors who want broad altcoin coverage and are comfortable with an execution-oriented workflow.

### 5. Messari

**Best for: Enterprise and institutional research teams — no longer a strong fit for retail**

Messari was once a go-to crypto research platform for retail investors. That changed in 2026. Following its Blockworks acquisition, Messari retired its Lite and Pro retail plans in June 2026 and pivoted toward enterprise clients.

**What it covers:** Institutional-grade crypto research, protocol intelligence, governance data.

**What it costs:** Enterprise pricing only as of mid-2026.

**Where it falls short:** Retail investors are no longer the target audience. If you were on a Messari Pro plan, you've likely already noticed the shift. Worth knowing before you spend time evaluating it.

**Who it's for:** Enterprise teams and funds. Not the right fit for self-directed retail investors in 2026.

### 6. Kaito AI

**Best for: Tracking Web3 social signals and narrative momentum**

Kaito AI aggregates social signals from across the Web3 ecosystem. It's useful for understanding which projects are gaining mindshare, which narratives are trending, and where attention is flowing in crypto communities.

**What it covers:** Web3 social signal aggregation, narrative tracking, attention metrics.

**What it costs:** Pricing varies by tier.

**Where it falls short:** Not a structured investment research tool. No meaningful on-chain data depth. No equities coverage. It tells you what people are talking about — not what the blockchain or market data actually shows.

**Who it's for:** Investors who want to track narrative momentum in crypto. Best used alongside a data-driven research tool, not as a standalone research layer.

### 7. TradingView

**Best for: Technical analysis across stocks and crypto with a large community**

TradingView is one of the most widely used charting platforms among retail investors. It covers both stocks and crypto, has an active community of published chart ideas, and offers screeners, alerts, and a broad asset universe.

**What it covers:** Charting and technical analysis for stocks and crypto, community ideas, screeners.

**What it costs:** Free tier available; paid plans start at $14.95/month.

**Where it falls short:** TradingView is a charting platform, not an AI research agent. It doesn't decode on-chain data. It doesn't run autonomous research. You're still doing the analytical work yourself — just with better charts.

**Who it's for:** Investors who rely heavily on technical analysis and want a well-established charting environment. A solid tool, but a different category from AI-driven research automation.
