## What Is an AI Stock Tracker, and How Does It Differ from a Stock Picker?

A tracker analyzes what you already hold. A picker scores or suggests stocks to buy. A robo-advisor goes further and recommends or executes changes for you.

Household stock ownership reached 58.7% in 2023, per the U.S. Federal Reserve as cited by Data Horizzon Research. A 2024 [Motley Fool survey](https://www.fool.com/research/survey-how-investors-are-using-generative-ai/) found that 66% of investors who use generative AI for investment decisions use ChatGPT.

## Best AI Stock Tracker Apps for Analyzing Your Portfolio

Barebone AI, PortfolioPilot, and AInvest are the best AI stock tracker apps for analyzing holdings you already own.

**1. Barebone AI.** It reviews your holdings through five lenses: growth, risk, income, sector balance, and momentum.
- **Best for:** investors who want a read-only portfolio checkup.
- **Access:** read-only broker sync, no trades.
- **Limitation:** mobile-first, with no desktop deep-dive terminal.

**2. PortfolioPilot.** This SEC-registered advisor analyzes connected portfolios for risk and suggests changes.
- **Best for:** investors who want risk guidance.
- **Access:** read-only; it cannot move money.
- **Limitation:** advice, not a live tracker.

**3. AInvest.** It offers portfolio analysis, trade signals, and an advisor agent named Aime.
- **Best for:** investors who want signals plus research.
- **Limitation:** focused on North American markets, and bullish or bearish signals sit in paid tiers.

## What Is the Best AI Prediction App for Stock Picks and Signals?

No app predicts winners reliably. These three score or suggest stocks, so use them as inputs, not instructions.

**4. Danelfin.** It gives each stock an explainable AI Score from 1 to 10, built on 900 technical, fundamental, and sentiment indicators.
- **Best for:** US and European stock screening.
- **Limitation:** scoring only; it alerts you when scores change.

**5. Prospero.ai.** It publishes daily picks based on options market data.
- **Best for:** short-term idea generation.
- **Limitation:** signals are picks, not tracking.

**6. StockPrompt.ai.** It answers natural language questions on Indian markets (NSE/BSE).
- **Limitation:** not relevant if you invest only in US stocks.

## Agenticly: A Live AI Stock Tracker App for Stocks and Crypto

**7.** [Agenticly](https://agenticly.app/) is an AI agent platform that analyzes real-time market data for self-directed investors in crypto and US equities. It decodes on-chain data (wallet flows, transaction volumes, network activity) for [bitcoin](https://agenticly.app/coin/bitcoin), Ethereum, and Solana, and it tracks US stock markets too. You can follow broader [market trends](https://agenticly.app/market-trends) there as well.

You schedule agents to run research while you do something else. They deliver conclusions, not raw data.

- **Best for:** self-directed investors who hold both stocks and crypto.
- **Limitation:** check Agenticly's site for pricing and broker integration details.

## Which Features Matter in an AI Stock Tracker, and What Does It Cost?

Compare these before you commit:

- **Broker sync:** does the app import your holdings automatically?
- **Access mode:** read-only is safer than trade-executing.
- **Risk metrics:** does it flag concentration and sector overlap?
- **Explainability:** does it show why a stock scores high?

Most apps here don't publish prices.

## Can an AI Stock Tracker Beat the Market?

No AI stock tracker reliably beats the market. MarketSenseAI returned 125.9% on S&P 100 stocks over 2023-2024 against the index's 73.5%, per [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/html/2502.00415v2). That's a research model, not a retail app, and it promises nothing for any app above.

## FAQs

### What is the best AI prediction app?
No app reliably predicts stock moves. Danelfin and Prospero.ai score and pick stocks, but treat their output as one input.

### Does AI trading pay real money?
Some research models beat indexes, but results don't transfer to retail apps. A 2025 survey cited on deeptracker.ai claims 78% of tracker users reported 15-20% performance gains, but that's a vendor-reported figure, not proof. Treat AI as research support, never a guaranteed income source.

### Is there an AI tool for stock trading?
Apps like AInvest and Prospero.ai generate signals and picks. PortfolioPilot and Barebone AI analyze without executing trades. Check each app's access mode before connecting a brokerage account.

### What stocks will rise with AI?
No tool can name them with certainty. AI scores and signals rank likelihoods based on past data, and markets change. Use them to shortlist ideas, then do your own research.

### Are AI stock trackers safe to connect to my broker?
Barebone AI and PortfolioPilot use read-only access, so they can't move money. For other apps, confirm the connection type before linking an account.

### Can an AI stock tracker beat the S&P 500?
A research model did over 2023-2024, but it's not a consumer app.

## Conclusion

Pick the tool by job. Use Barebone AI or PortfolioPilot to analyze holdings, Danelfin or Prospero.ai to find ideas, and Agenticly if you track stocks and crypto together.

Start by writing down what you need: a portfolio checkup, stock ideas, or both. Then confirm read-only access before you link any broker.
