If you logged into Messari recently expecting to renew your Lite or Pro subscription, you already know. The plans are gone. Messari retired both retail tiers in June 2026 following its acquisition by Blockworks, pivoting hard toward enterprise and institutional clients.

For retail investors who relied on Messari for crypto research, that's a real problem. Here's what happened, what the current alternatives actually offer, and which one makes sense depending on what you need.

## Why Messari Dropped Its Retail Plans

Messari built a reputation as one of the more serious crypto research platforms available to individual investors. The Lite and Pro plans gave retail investors access to screeners, project profiles, and analyst reports at a price that was at least within reach.

The Blockworks acquisition changed that. Blockworks is a media and intelligence company with deep institutional roots in crypto coverage. After the deal closed, Messari's roadmap shifted toward enterprise data contracts and institutional research. The Lite and Pro plans were retired in June 2026.

The message to retail investors was clear: this platform is no longer built for you.

## What the Remaining Options Look Like

If you're a self-directed investor managing both crypto and US stocks, here's an honest look at what's left.

### Glassnode

Glassnode is the benchmark for on-chain data depth. It covers Bitcoin and Ethereum with over 1,500 metrics and up to 15 years of historical data. If you want to understand miner behavior, exchange flows, or long-term holder patterns, it's genuinely strong.

The problems: crypto-only, no US equities, and no automation whatsoever. You pull the charts, you draw the conclusions. The Advanced plan runs $49 per month on an annual basis; Professional pricing is custom.

If you have the time and analytical background to work through raw on-chain data, Glassnode delivers. Most retail investors have neither.

### Nansen

Nansen focuses on wallet labeling and smart money flow tracking across Ethereum and Solana — useful for identifying where large wallets are moving funds. The Pioneer plan runs roughly $120 per year; Professional is $1,200 per year.

No equities coverage. No agent automation. Its Trustpilot average sits at 2.9 out of 5. The platform is primarily designed for on-chain power users, not investors who want research delivered to them.

### Token Metrics

Token Metrics offers AI-generated ratings across more than 6,000 crypto projects and integrates on-chain trade execution via LiFi, starting at around $49 per month. Useful if you're focused on crypto project evaluation and want execution built in.

But it's crypto-only and oriented around execution rather than scheduled research. If you hold US equities alongside your crypto positions, that side of your portfolio simply doesn't exist here.

### Kaito AI

Kaito aggregates social signals from across Web3, which can surface emerging narratives before they hit mainstream feeds. Genuinely useful for tracking sentiment and attention flows.

It's not a structured investment research tool, though. No on-chain data depth, no equities coverage, and it doesn't produce the kind of portfolio-specific analysis that Messari's retail plans once offered.

Here's the honest problem: every one of these platforms was built for one thing. Glassnode is for on-chain analysts. Nansen is for wallet trackers. Token Metrics is for crypto execution. Kaito is for social signal monitoring.
