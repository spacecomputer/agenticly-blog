## What Glassnode Does

Glassnode is a dedicated on-chain analytics platform covering Bitcoin and Ethereum. It tracks over 1,500 on-chain metrics — miner flows, exchange balances, realized cap, long-term holder behavior — with up to 15 years of historical data on higher tiers.

On-chain data means information recorded directly on a blockchain: how much Bitcoin is moving between wallets, how much is sitting on exchanges, how active large holders have been. Glassnode has built one of the deepest libraries of these metrics available to retail investors.

The Advanced plan runs $49 per month billed annually. Professional pricing is custom, which in practice means it's aimed at institutional clients.

### Where Glassnode Falls Short for Retail Investors

Glassnode is a data library, not a research engine. You log in, navigate to a chart, interpret what you see, and draw your own conclusions. That takes time and analytical skill. There's no automation. No agents running research on your behalf while you sleep.

It also covers only Bitcoin and Ethereum. Solana isn't there. US equities aren't anywhere near its scope. If you hold tech stocks alongside your crypto positions, Glassnode has nothing to offer on that side of your portfolio.

The platform is web-only and desktop-oriented. It wasn't built for the investor checking markets on a phone between meetings.

## What Agenticly Does

[Agenticly](https://agenticly.app/) is a mobile-first AI analytics app for iOS and Android. Rather than handing you raw charts to interpret, it lets you deploy autonomous AI agents that do the research for you.

Those agents decode on-chain data for Bitcoin, Ethereum, and Solana — tracking blockchain-level activity that reveals what large holders and active wallets are actually doing, not just what the price is doing. At the same time, the same agents monitor US equity markets, so your entire portfolio lives inside one research workflow.

The defining feature is scheduling. Set an agent to run on a recurring basis and it delivers personalized insights automatically. You wake up to analysis, not raw data. Stop sifting through market noise. Let your agents do it.

Agenticly is freemium. You can get started without paying anything upfront through the free tier at [agenticly.app](https://agenticly.app/).
