Nansen built its reputation on wallet labeling and smart money tracking. If you've spent any time in on-chain analytics, you've almost certainly come across it. But if you're a retail investor, you've probably also hit the same wall: $1,200 per year for the Professional plan, a Trustpilot average of 2.9 out of 5, and a platform that still only covers crypto with no US equities anywhere in sight.

That's a lot to pay for a tool that leaves half your portfolio unresearched.

This article breaks down the real Nansen alternatives available in 2026—what each one actually does well, where each falls short, and which makes the most sense depending on how you invest.

## Why Investors Are Looking for Nansen Alternatives in 2026

Nansen is genuinely useful for tracking Ethereum and Solana wallet flows. If you want to see where large wallets are moving funds, it does that well. But several things have pushed retail investors to look elsewhere.

**The price is steep for what you get.** At $1,200 per year, the Professional plan is built for funds and power users—not someone managing a personal portfolio.

**It's crypto-only.** If you hold US equities alongside your crypto positions, Nansen offers nothing on that side of the ledger. You're still logging into a separate platform for stock research.

**It's a passive dashboard.** Nansen shows you data. It doesn't interpret it, alert you proactively, or run automated research while you're away from your screen.

**No agent scheduling.** Every insight requires you to log in, navigate the interface, and manually draw conclusions. That's fine if data analysis is your hobby. It's a real problem if you have a job.

## The Main Nansen Alternatives Worth Considering

### Glassnode

Glassnode is the most data-rich on-chain analytics platform available. With over 1,500 on-chain metrics and up to 15 years of historical data, it's the go-to for serious Bitcoin and Ethereum analysis.

The Advanced plan runs $49 per month billed annually—considerably more accessible than Nansen's Professional pricing.

The catch: Glassnode is crypto-only, requires real analytical expertise to extract value, and has no AI agent scheduling. You still need to know what you're looking for and how to interpret what you find. For investors who want answers rather than raw data, the learning curve is significant.

### Token Metrics

Token Metrics provides AI-generated ratings across more than 6,000 crypto projects and includes on-chain trade execution through LiFi integration, starting at around $49 per month.

It's a reasonable option if your portfolio is entirely crypto-focused and you want AI-assisted ratings to guide decisions. But it covers no US equities, and there's no research-agent scheduling layer. You're still working with a static tool that requires manual engagement.

### Messari

Messari was a solid option for research-oriented investors for several years. That changed in June 2026, when Messari retired its Lite and Pro plans following its Blockworks acquisition and moved to enterprise-only pricing.

If you were a Messari Pro subscriber, you've already had to find something else. And even when retail plans existed, Messari never covered equities or offered any form of automated research.

### Kaito AI

Kaito aggregates Web3 social signals and is useful for tracking sentiment and narrative momentum across crypto communities. It's not a structured investment research tool—no equities coverage, and no on-chain data depth comparable to Glassnode or Nansen.

It serves a different purpose. If you want to know what crypto Twitter is talking about, Kaito is relevant. If you're trying to make informed decisions across a hybrid portfolio, it's not the right fit.

## FAQs

**Is Nansen worth $1,200 per year for a retail investor?**

For most retail investors, no. The Professional plan is priced for institutional use cases. If you're managing a personal portfolio and want on-chain insights, the cost-to-value ratio is hard to justify—especially when the platform covers no US equities and requires manual interpretation throughout.

**What is the best Nansen alternative for someone who also holds US stocks?**

No other tool in the Nansen, Glassnode, or Messari tier covers both on-chain crypto and US equities in one place. Agenticly is currently the only consumer-facing option that combines both alongside AI agent scheduling.

**Does Glassnode replace Nansen?**

Glassnode and Nansen serve overlapping but distinct purposes. Glassnode is stronger for deep on-chain metrics and Bitcoin analysis. Nansen is stronger for wallet labeling and smart money flow tracking on Ethereum and Solana. Neither replaces the other, and neither covers US equities or offers automated research agents.

**What happened to Messari's retail plans?**

Messari retired its Lite and Pro plans in June 2026 following its Blockworks acquisition. The platform moved to enterprise-only pricing, which effectively removes it as an option for retail investors.

**Can I use multiple tools together instead of switching?**

You can, and many investors do. The downside is cost and fragmentation. Running Glassnode alongside a stock research tool and manually synthesizing the outputs is exactly the workflow that scheduled AI agents are designed to replace.

**What does "scheduled AI agents" actually mean in practice?**

Rather than logging into a platform and manually reviewing charts, you configure an agent to research specific markets or signals on a set schedule. The agent runs automatically and delivers conclusions—so you get actionable insights without spending time on data collection yourself.
