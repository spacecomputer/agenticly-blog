## Trade Ideas: What Makes It the Benchmark Stock Scanner?

Trade Ideas earns its benchmark status through scanning depth and AI specificity. As reported by tradealgo.com in 2026, it runs more than 60 customizable scan types across more than 8,000 stocks in real-time. That breadth, combined with structured AI output, is hard to match.

Holly AI is Trade Ideas' built-in AI system. It runs overnight analysis and delivers a structured output each morning: 3 to 8 trade ideas per day, each with a specific entry point, target, and stop-loss level. That specificity matters. You are not getting a probability score or a pattern flag. You are getting a complete trade setup.

Trade Ideas is a Windows-centric desktop application. Mac users need a workaround or accept a web version with fewer features. There is no mobile app. The learning curve is steep, and the pricing sits firmly in the premium tier, aimed at active traders who trade frequently enough to justify the cost.

Two other friction points are worth knowing before you commit. First, Trade Ideas covers U.S. stocks and ETFs only. If you trade crypto, forex, or futures, this tool does not help you there. Second, all sales are final. No refund policy means the trial period, if any, is your only window to test whether the platform fits your workflow.

For a high-frequency day trader focused on U.S. equities, Trade Ideas is hard to beat. For anyone else, the cost-to-coverage ratio starts to look unfavorable.

## TrendSpider: Best Alternative for Automated Charting

TrendSpider runs entirely in the cloud, so there is nothing to download and no OS workaround required. It automates the charting work that most technical traders do manually: trendline detection, pattern recognition, and backtesting, all without writing a line of code.

The key difference from Trade Ideas is the workflow it supports. Trade Ideas is built for discovery, finding stocks that meet specific criteria right now. TrendSpider is built for analysis, taking a stock you already have in mind and systematically testing whether your read on it holds up historically. Those are different jobs.

TrendSpider also covers more asset classes. Stocks, crypto, forex, and futures all run on the same platform. If your portfolio spans more than U.S. equities, that breadth matters. Per wethetraders.com and aitradingcamp.com, TrendSpider is better suited to technical traders who want to systematize their charting process than to traders looking for a real-time discovery scanner.

The price sits in the budget tier, well below Trade Ideas. For a swing trader who already has a watchlist and wants to validate setups faster, TrendSpider is the stronger fit. For a day trader who needs a live scanner surfacing new opportunities every minute, it is a partial replacement at best.

## Tickeron: Best Alternative for AI-Driven Trade Ideas

Tickeron uses an AI Advisor and a Pattern Search Engine to generate trade ideas based on statistical probabilities and technical indicators. It runs in the cloud, covers stocks, ETFs, and crypto, and prices in the budget tier.

The comparison to Holly AI is instructive. Holly AI delivers a specific trade setup: entry, target, stop-loss. Tickeron delivers a probability-weighted signal: this pattern has resolved in a particular direction X percent of the time. Both are useful, but they suit different decision styles. If you want the AI to hand you a complete plan, Holly AI is more direct. If you want to weigh odds and make your own call, Tickeron fits better.

[According to a 2024 Motley Fool survey of 2,000 investors](https://www.fool.com/research/survey-how-investors-are-using-generative-ai/), 69% of those using AI tools reported saving time on stock research. Tickeron's AI Advisor is the kind of tool that drives that result: it handles the pattern-matching work so you can focus on the decision.

Tickeron also includes specialized scans for Bollinger Bands, Volume Leaders, and Smart Beta ETFs, plus a free Trade Ideas Scanner page with basic functionality. That free tier is the lowest-friction entry point among the three tools covered here. If you want to test AI-assisted scanning before paying anything, Tickeron is where to start.

## Which Stock Scanner Is Best for Your Trading Style?

The right tool depends on what you actually do, not on which tool has the most features. There is no shortage of options. The question is fit.

Mobile access and free pricing are the two most common reasons traders look elsewhere. None of the three tools here offers a full-featured mobile app. Trade Ideas is desktop-only. TrendSpider and Tickeron run in the browser, which works on mobile but is not optimized for it. If you need a polished mobile experience, all three fall short.

Free access is another common question. Check each tool's current website to confirm what free or trial access is available, as these policies change. Among the three, Tickeron offers the most accessible entry point through its free Trade Ideas Scanner page.

## When an AI Agent Platform Is the Better Choice for Market Research

Stock scanners solve a specific problem: finding U.S. equities that meet technical criteria right now. That is a real and valuable job. But it is not the only research job traders have.

If your portfolio includes crypto alongside equities, you already know the fragmentation problem. You run a stock scanner for equities, check a separate crypto data platform, and try to synthesize two different data streams into one decision. That is not a scanner problem. That is a coverage problem.

[Agenticly](https://agenticly.app/) is an AI agent platform that analyzes real-time market data across both U.S. equities and cryptocurrencies. It decodes on-chain data (wallet flows, transaction volumes, network activity on Bitcoin, Ethereum, and Solana) alongside standard stock market data, and delivers personalized insights across both asset classes. You can schedule agents to run research automatically, so the analysis happens in the background while you focus on other things.

The difference from a traditional scanner is the output type. A scanner surfaces a list of stocks meeting criteria. An agent processes that data and returns a summary you can act on directly. You decide what to do with it.

This matters most for three types of traders: those who actively manage both crypto and equity positions, those who want automated research without building their own screener logic, and those who find the learning curve on professional-grade scanners too steep for the value they get out of it.

If your research needs stay within U.S. equities and you trade frequently, Trade Ideas or one of its alternatives above is the right starting point. If your needs go wider, an agent platform covers ground that no stock scanner was built to reach.

## FAQs

### How much does Trade Ideas scanner cost?

Trade Ideas sits in the premium price tier. The no-refund policy means you should treat any trial period as your real evaluation window.

### Does Trade Ideas offer a free trial or free version?

Check the Trade Ideas website for current free and trial access options, as these policies change. Among the alternatives here, Tickeron offers the most accessible free entry point through its free Trade Ideas Scanner page, which includes basic scanning functionality.

### Is there a Trade Ideas mobile app?

No. Trade Ideas is a desktop application, primarily built for Windows. Mac users need a workaround or accept a reduced-feature web version. TrendSpider and Tickeron both run in the browser and are accessible on mobile, but neither has a dedicated mobile app optimized for smaller screens.

### What is Holly AI in Trade Ideas?

Holly AI is Trade Ideas' built-in AI system. It runs analysis overnight and delivers 3 to 8 structured trade ideas each morning, with specific entry points, price targets, and stop-loss levels for U.S. stocks.

### Can TrendSpider or Tickeron replace Trade Ideas for day trading?

For day trading specifically, neither is a direct replacement. TrendSpider is better suited to charting and backtesting than live scanning. Tickeron's AI signals are probability-weighted rather than real-time discovery alerts. If live U.S. stock scanning is your primary need, the cost is the main reason to look at alternatives.

## Conclusion

Trade Ideas carries a premium price, a desktop-only setup, and U.S.-equity-only coverage, which rules it out for a significant portion of traders. TrendSpider covers automated charting and multi-asset analysis at a lower cost. Tickeron offers AI-assisted signals with a free entry tier.

If your research spans both stocks and crypto, Tickeron covers both, but the depth of crypto analysis varies across tools. Decide first which asset classes you actually trade, then match that to the tool's coverage. That single filter will eliminate most of the wrong options before you spend time on a trial.
