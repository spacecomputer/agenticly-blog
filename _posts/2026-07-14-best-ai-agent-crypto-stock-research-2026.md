### Glassnode

Glassnode is the most established on-chain analytics platform for Bitcoin and Ethereum. With more than 1,500 on-chain metrics and up to 15 years of historical data, it's genuinely powerful for deep crypto research.

The catch: it's crypto-only, covers only Bitcoin and Ethereum (not Solana), and has no AI agent layer. Everything requires manual interpretation. You pull the charts, you draw the conclusions. That works if you have the time and the analytical background — but it's a significant workload.

Pricing starts at $49 per month on the Advanced plan (billed annually), with Professional pricing on request.

**Best for:** Experienced crypto analysts who want deep historical on-chain data for Bitcoin and Ethereum and are comfortable doing their own interpretation.

**Limitations:** No Solana coverage, no US equities, no AI automation, and a heavy manual lift.

### Nansen

Nansen focuses on wallet labeling and smart money flow tracking across Ethereum and Solana. If you want to see where large wallets are moving funds, it does that well.

It doesn't cover US equities and has no agent scheduling or AI automation layer. The Pioneer plan runs roughly $120 per year; the Professional plan is $1,200 per year. Its Trustpilot average sits at 2.9 out of 5, which reflects some frustration with the product experience relative to the price.

**Best for:** Ethereum and Solana investors who specifically want wallet tracking and smart money flow data.

**Limitations:** No equities coverage, no agent automation, crypto-only, and mixed user satisfaction at higher price points.

### Token Metrics

Token Metrics offers AI-generated ratings across more than 6,000 crypto projects and integrates on-chain trade execution through its LiFi connection. It's built more around execution than scheduled research, starting at roughly $49 per month.

It's crypto-only — no US equities coverage, no scheduled agent architecture. The AI layer is real, but it's oriented toward ratings and trade signals rather than autonomous research delivery.

**Best for:** Crypto-focused investors who want AI-assisted ratings and are interested in on-chain execution features.

**Limitations:** No equities, no scheduling, and more execution-focused than research-focused.

### Messari

Messari was a go-to platform for retail crypto research for several years. That changed in 2026. Following its acquisition by Blockworks, Messari retired its Lite and Pro retail plans in June 2026 and pivoted toward enterprise clients.

If you were relying on Messari for accessible crypto research, that option is effectively gone. Enterprise positioning means it's no longer built for self-directed retail investors.

**Best for:** Enterprise clients and institutional teams.

**Limitations:** Retail plans discontinued as of June 2026. Not a realistic option for individual investors.

### Kaito AI

Kaito AI aggregates social signals from across Web3, which can be useful for tracking narrative momentum and community sentiment. It's not a structured investment research tool — no on-chain data depth, no equities coverage.

Think of it as a social intelligence layer, not a research platform. Useful as a supplement, not a primary source.

**Best for:** Investors who want to track Web3 social sentiment and narrative trends.

**Limitations:** Not a research platform, no on-chain data depth, no equities, no agent automation.
