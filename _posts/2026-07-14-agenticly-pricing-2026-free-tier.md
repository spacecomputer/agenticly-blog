## How Agenticly's Free Tier Compares to Paid Alternatives

Here's what retail investors are paying for comparable research tools in 2026 — and what they're still not getting.

### Glassnode

Glassnode offers over 1,500 on-chain metrics for Bitcoin and Ethereum, with up to 15 years of historical data. The Advanced plan runs $49 per month billed annually. Professional pricing is custom.

The catch: crypto only. No equities. No agent automation. You get the raw data, but making sense of it is entirely on you. If you're not already fluent in on-chain analytics, the dashboard can feel like staring at a Bloomberg terminal with no context.

### Nansen

Nansen focuses on wallet labeling and smart money flow tracking across Ethereum and Solana. Pioneer plan is roughly $120 per year; Professional is $1,200 per year.

No equities coverage. No scheduled agent runs. You're manually checking dashboards to find signals. Its Trustpilot average sits at 2.9 out of 5 — which suggests the experience doesn't always justify the price.

### Token Metrics

Token Metrics provides AI-generated ratings across 6,000-plus crypto projects and integrates on-chain trade execution, starting around $49 per month. Crypto only, and focused on execution rather than recurring research delivery. If you want agents running scheduled analysis across both crypto and stocks, this isn't that.

### Messari

Messari was a reliable option for retail investors who wanted structured crypto research. That changed in June 2026, when Messari retired its Lite and Pro retail plans following its acquisition by Blockworks. The platform has shifted toward enterprise clients. Retail investors who relied on it are now looking for somewhere else to go.

### Kaito AI

Kaito aggregates Web3 social signals and is useful for tracking narrative momentum. It's not a structured investment research tool. There's no meaningful on-chain data depth for Bitcoin, Ethereum, or Solana, and no equities layer.
