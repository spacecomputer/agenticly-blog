## What Kaito AI Actually Does

Kaito AI is a Web3 intelligence platform built around social and narrative signals. It indexes content from X, Discord, Telegram, and other crypto-native channels to surface which projects, tokens, and topics are gaining attention.

It doesn't generate personalized research based on your portfolio. And there's no agent automation layer running analysis on your behalf on a schedule.

Kaito tells you what people are talking about. It doesn't tell you what the data actually shows.

### Why Social Signals Alone Fall Short

Social signals are a lagging indicator dressed up as a leading one. By the time a narrative is trending on X or Telegram, a lot of the smart money has already positioned. That's not always true — but it's true often enough to matter.

On-chain data gives you a different angle. When large Bitcoin wallets start moving coins to exchanges, that's a measurable signal. When Ethereum gas fees spike in a specific pattern, that reflects real activity. When Solana's active address count diverges from its price, that's worth knowing. These signals are rooted in what participants are actually doing — and they rarely show up in social feeds until after the fact.
